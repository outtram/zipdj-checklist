Get good files, not a big mess

Exact mix first. Club/extended versions often give you more mixing room, but the radio edit may be the song you actually love. Match artist, title, version and duration. A familiar title is not enough.

Quality needs a listen. A 320 kbps label is not proof of a clean master. Listen for clipping, thin sound, odd edits and damaged intros/outros. Do not upscale a low-quality file and call it fixed.

Make the library work. Analyse BPM/key, check the beatgrid, add cues, then tag worthiness, energy and role. Build sets from files that are present and auditioned. Spotify is the inspiration layer, not a promise of a matching MP3.
